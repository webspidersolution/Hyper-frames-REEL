## Intent

A ~50 s Instagram Reel (9:16) plus a 16:9 cut for **Esha Bargate Productions** (eshabargateproductions.com) on
"Why Audience Research Should Begin Before Production". It is aimed at founders, marketing heads and brand managers who
commission video, and it sells EBP's strategy / audience-research service. The user's own words: "first research …
video should have vo from elevenlabs and image real logo from esha and if u need any assets use openart mcp gpt img 2.5
1k low quality". The full plan, research and sources are in `plans/ebp-research-before-production/PLAN.md` (repo root).

## Assets

- `assets/audio/vo_source.mp3` — ElevenLabs VO generated by the user: Sarah – Approachable and Informative, Eleven
  Multilingual v2, speed 1.05, stability 50, similarity 75, style 5, speaker boost. Transcribed with Parakeet into
  `vo_transcript.json` and placed on the beat grid by `tools/vo.py` → `vo.wav`, `words.json`, `captions.srt/json`.
- `assets/brand/ebp-logo-source.png` — the real logo from eshabargateproductions.com/images/assets/erp-logo-transparent.png
  (534×772). `tools/prep_brand.py` un-mattes only the outer edge halo, keeps the white circuit traces, and splits it
  into `foot-l.png` / `foot-r.png` (+ `logo-meta.json`).
- `assets/photos/p1…p5-*.jpg` (JPEG q93 of the PNG originals) — photo plates generated on OpenArt with **GPT Image 2.5 Flare**, text2image, 1K, quality
  low (5 credits each, 10 images = 50 credits). The prompts are in PLAN.md §8. P1 empty screening room, P2 sound-stage
  floor with a red T-mark, P3 defocused film set (behind the 3D clapperboard), P4 script page ("on paper"), P5 big film
  set ("after the shoot"). No AI-rendered text is used on screen.
- `assets/fonts/` — Space Grotesk 500/700 (display) and Inter 400–700 (UI), the brand's site fonts. OFL, from Google Fonts.
- `assets/tex/grain.png` — hyperreel starter grain.
- SFX — synthesized in `tools/sfx.py` (step, slate clap, chalk, freeze, pops, clicks, shimmer, count) plus Pixabay-licensed
  one-shots bundled with media-use (whoosh-cinematic, whoosh-short, impact-bass-1, sparkle). The score is synthesized by
  `tools/score.py`.

## Customizations

- Built with /hyperreel: Three.js hero (clapperboard) + HTML type, original synthesized score, code + library SFX,
  -14 LUFS.
- Formats: 9x16 (root), 16x9.
- Tempo 134.5 bpm (bar = 1.7844 s), chosen so every scene cut lands on a bar line while the VO keeps its natural
  pauses.
