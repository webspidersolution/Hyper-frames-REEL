## Design

- **Concept angle (the user's script):** a business juggling too many agencies, tabs and tools, scattered across
  platforms, until WebSpider connects it all: one goal in, growth paths out, Google + AI search handled, ads/SEO/social
  woven into one web. One team to plan, launch and grow.
- **Mode:** launch-video-clone *Rebrand*. The cut frames, move types, easing and transitions follow BREAKDOWN.md
  (the reference's measured timing). Every picture, word and sound is new and on-brand: no reference artwork,
  photos, copy, logos or music are reused.
- **Type:** ui-showcase + abstract-diagram hybrid, with a Three.js glossy spring-green hero (the brand kit's "one
  glossy Spring Green hero object").
- **Palette (WSS Brandkit v1.1):** Cream `#f2f3ef` (light grounds), Slate `#5a5e64` (text on light), Ink `#141618`
  (dark grounds, editor), Spring Green `#92c131` (the one accent: a word, a sticker, a node, the hero), Leaf `#7fa042`
  / Olive `#6d7f53` (shades of the accent). One warm-red warning tone only inside the error moment (UI content).
- **Fonts:** Poppins 600/500 (display, UI), Arimo 400/700 (small UI labels), JetBrains Mono (code). The logo is the
  real `wss-logo.svg` / `wss-icon.svg`, revealed, never retyped; white version on photos (brand rule).
- **Background roles:** dark: depth-of-field search shards + a breathing green glow; light: paper grain + guide lines;
  photo plates (OpenArt GPT Image 2.5, text-free): sea horizon at dawn, misty pine forest, mustard-blossom field under
  sky. Grain over everything.

## As built (after review rounds)

- **VO**: the user's ElevenLabs take 3, cut into 14 phrases at measured pauses (`tools/vo.py`); every on-screen line
  rises on its spoken word (`timeline.json` → `cues`).
- **Timing**: shots 1–13 on the reference's exact cut frames; shot 14 runs 21.07–28.0 s and shot 15 28.0–35.0 s to fit
  "Bring ads… scale what matters." and "One team to plan, launch, and grow. WebSpider Solutions."
- **Real platform marks** (user request): Google Ads / Meta Ads / Search Console / Google / Instagram / WordPress on the
  network nodes; Google, Google Ads, Meta, ChatGPT + Gemini + Perplexity, WordPress, Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube,
  Search Console, Analytics on the growth-path cards and web chips; real favicons on the "Too many tabs" shards; the
  Google G in "Rank on Google," and the three AI assistants in "show up in AI search,".
- **Shot 15** builds as two lines: "One team to" / "plan, launch, and grow." with one green sticker that hops to each
  new word, then the white lockup + webspidersolutions.com.
