## motion-reel (local skill)
The beat-grid-first workflow, the synthesized score and SFX approach, the -14 LUFS mix and the critique loop come from
the user's local `/motion-reel` skill (`~/.claude/skills/motion-reel`). Check its terms before publishing this skill.

## Sound library
`scripts/sfx.py` reads one-shots from `HYPERREEL_SFX_LIB` (default `D:\Hyperframes\sfx`, an `index.json` plus mp3s:
the Pixabay-licensed HyperFrames set and the HeyGen catalog). Check each library's license for the client's use.
